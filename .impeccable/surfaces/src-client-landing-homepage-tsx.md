# Hosted landing page (Persuade)

Scope: the hosted service's public pages: `/` for signed-out visitors and `/home`, plus `/terms` and `/privacy`. Visitor mode: Persuade (legal pages: Read). Audience: someone already printing for other people, evaluating whether to self-host or use the hosted version. Action: copy the Docker command, or create a free workspace. Proof: the running product (real screenshots from the demo print-farm workspace) and an interactive copy-cell ticket. Constraints: facts from `src/core`; product boundary stated; no testimonials or metrics; self-hosted assets only.

World: the app's own Pass (DESIGN.md), not a separate marketing world. Run unattended (subagent, no question tool): the dealt lead structure was built without a decision round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is the pass seen from the counter: the offer is hung as tickets on a rail, and every claim is backed by the running board. Refuses a split hero over a row of equal icon cards, and refuses a marketing palette the app does not have.

OWN-WORLD: Brushed-steel ground (night steel in dark), ticket paper with a stage bar and rail hanger, dashed perforations, ink-black command docket, thermal red only on the one commit action, the five stage colours as flat fills owning the stage legend. Oswald condensed uppercase for ticket names and section heads, IBM Plex Sans for prose, IBM Plex Mono for commands, counts, and ticket headers.

STORY: The visitor learns in one viewport that this is a free, open-source print queue they can run themselves, sees the real board, and copies the Docker command or creates a hosted workspace. Further down: per-copy tracking, catalogues, quotes, optional usage and stock, storage, plans, and what it will not do.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App-style steel bar (brand, links, outline Create a free workspace). A steel rail across the page; hanging from it the lead ticket (stage bar, the H1 at display size, lede, five stage cells, perforation, a stub with Free · AGPL-3.0 and the red Create a free workspace) and beside it the ink Docker docket with Copy, the self-hosting guide, and TrueNAS and Unraid links. The offer returns as the same rail in Run it your way. Below the rail, the real board screenshot begins.

FORM: Spec-sheet lines in the Pass world (heading and purpose in a margin column beside a steel plate of evidence), position 4 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:1a81418db80c1285. Raise from the sticker-album spread: what the product refuses to do is drawn as empty ghost slots, dashed outlines that say who does that job instead.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the copy ticket. A request for six copies whose cells restamp in the next stage colour each time the visitor advances a copy, exactly as on the board.
